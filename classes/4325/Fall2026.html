What can a computer compute?

Explore the mathematical foundations of computation, from finite automata and formal languages to Turing machines, computability, and complexity.

Textbook: Introduction to the Theory of Computation, Michael Sipser, 3rd edition.

Schedule & topics

Topics include finite-state machines, pushdown automata, formal grammars, Turing machines, the halting problem, and computational complexity.
