Welcome

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley. My research focuses on unconventional models of computation, including chemical reaction networks, molecular computing, and algorithmic self-assembly.

My Bio

I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Texas at Austin in August 2024. Before that, I completed my M.S. in Computer Science at the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley in May 2020 and my B.S. in Computer Science there in May 2018.

Opportunities

I am currently recruiting motivated PhD students to join my research group at UTRGV. The ideal candidate is intellectually curious and self-driven, with a strong interest in abstract theoretical models of computation. A background in algorithms, discrete mathematics, and formal proofs is preferred but not strictly required.

If you are interested, please email me with a brief introduction, your CV, and any relevant research experience or coursework.
